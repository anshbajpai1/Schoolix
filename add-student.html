Admission workflow

The student is added to the school collection, section is assigned automatically, roll numbers are refreshed and the monthly fee for the current month is created as an imposed entry.

Payment handling

Partial payments stay attached to the same fee entry. If an amount higher than the due balance is received, the extra amount is recorded separately and can be adjusted to any future month.
